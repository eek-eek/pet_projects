## 1. Bank of Russia (Банк России) statistics: brokerage clients, funded vs. zero or small accounts, size distribution, concentration, margin trading, IIS

### Takeaway
In mid-2026 Russia had about 41.9 mn unique retail investors registered on MOEX, equal to 55% of the economically active population. Only about 5.9 mn of them (about 14%) held more than 10k RUB. Retail broker assets were 13.6 trn RUB at end-Q2 2026, which works out to about 2.3 mn RUB per funded client. Assets are extremely concentrated: in 2025, investors with 100 mn RUB or more held over 40% of positions, while half of investors had portfolios of 100k RUB or less.

### Inferences
- **C1 [computed] Funded share of registered investors:** 5.9 / 41.9 = **14.1%** at end-Q2 2026. At end-2025, 5.5 / 40.1 = **13.7%**. So about **86%** of registered unique investors hold 10k RUB or less (zero or small). This is the core emerging-market feature of the funnel.
- **C2 [computed] Broker relationships per unique investor:** 59 / 41.9 = **1.41**. This is multi-homing. Market share measured in "clients" overstates the true footprint.
- **C3 [computed] Assets per client:** per funded client, 13.6 trn / 5.9 mn = **2.31 mn RUB** (Q2 2026). At end-2025, 12.3 trn / 5.5 mn = **2.24 mn RUB**. Per registered unique client, 13.6 trn / 41.9 mn = **325k RUB**. The mean is far above the median: half of investors hold 100k RUB or less (source E).
- **C4 [computed] Inflow intensity:** Q2 2026, 1.1 / 13.6 = **8.1%** of period-end assets, or about 32% annualised (x4). Q1 2026, 0.91 / 13.3 = **6.8%**. FY2025, 2.5 / 12.3 = **20.3%**.
- **C5 [computed] Market (revaluation) effect:** Q2 2026, assets rose 13.6 − 13.3 = +0.3 trn while the inflow was 1.1 trn. That implies about **−0.8 trn** of revaluation and other effects, or about −6.0% of opening assets (−0.8 / 13.3). This matches the CBR's comment about negative revaluation of Russian equities. Q1 2026: 13.3 − 12.3 − 0.91 = **+0.09 trn**, roughly flat. All inputs are rounded to 0.1 trn, so allow about ±0.15 trn.
- **C6 [computed] Implied economically active population:** 41.9 / 0.55 ≈ **76 mn**.
- **C7 [computed] Empty accounts at end-Q1 2026:** 0.63 × 57 mn ≈ **35.9 mn**.
- **C8 [computed] IIS:**
  - Average IIS including empty accounts: 993 bn / 6.6 mn = **150k RUB**.
  - Funded IIS: 26% × 6.5 mn = 1.69 mn. That gives 969 bn / 1.69 mn = **573k RUB** per funded IIS, consistent with the reported 561k.
  - IIS-3 share of all IIS: 1.6 / 6.5 = **24.6%**.
  - IIS share of all retail broker assets: 993 bn / 13.6 trn = **7.3%**.
- **C9 [computed] Q2 2026 inflow per funded client:** 1.1 trn / 5.9 mn ≈ **186k RUB per quarter**.
- **Model implication:** use a three-step funnel.
  - Step 1: registered or unique investors = 100%.
  - Step 2: funded investors (over 10k RUB) ≈ 14%.
  - Step 3: monthly traders ≈ 7–9% of registered, which is ≈ 50–60% of funded (see section 2).
  - Concentration on top of the funnel: roughly the top 0.05–1% of clients hold 36–74% of assets (dated 2021–22 data), and the 100 mn RUB+ tier held over 40% of positions in 2025.
- High-rate period: bonds were 36–38% of retail assets, and bonds plus money-market funds dominate inflows. Revenue models should weight interest on client cash and bond placement fees above equity commissions (see section 4).

### Gaps
- **Method:** primary PDFs (CBR reviews, the CBR retail-market report) could not be opened because of the egress block, and search ran out. Asset-size bucket tables (for example 0, up to 10k, 10k–100k, 100k–1 mn, 1–10 mn, 10–100 mn, over 100 mn RUB) and up-to-date 2025–26 shares of assets for the top 1% and top 10% were **not retrieved**. The latest hard concentration numbers are from 2021–22, apart from the qualitative "over 40% of positions" for 2025.
- One search summary said "the share of active investors among clients excluding small accounts grew to 37%" in Q2 2026. The definition is unclear, so it is not used. Check it in the PDF.
- Margin trading: no 2025–26 aggregate (retail uncovered positions in RUB, number of margin users, share of clients) was found.
- The CBR counts of active clients, meaning clients with at least one trade in the quarter, were not retrieved. MOEX monthly-trader counts are used as a proxy instead (section 2).
- The ABN figures (57 mn accounts, "56 mn a year earlier") conflict with the CBR's growth rates. The definitions of "accounts" and "clients" differ.

## 2. Moscow Exchange: registered vs. monthly active retail investors, retail share of trading, number of investors trading, average trade size

### Takeaway
MOEX had 42.5 mn retail investors with brokerage accounts in August 2026, but only about 3.0–3.5 mn trade in a given month (about 7–9%), and about a quarter trade at least once a year. Retail investors are the majority of equity turnover (about 63–71%) but a minority of bond turnover. Their net buying is dominated by bonds and funds.

### Inferences
- **[computed] Monthly active rate:**
  - August 2026: 3.0 / 42.5 = **7.1%**. This is a lower bound, because the source says "over 3 mn".
  - 2025 average, using year-end registrations: 3.5 / 40.1 = **8.7%**.

- **[computed] Break-even turnover for T "Трейдер" vs. "Инвестор":** fee / (0.30% − 0.05%). At 290 RUB that is **116k RUB per month**; at 390 RUB it is **156k RUB per month**. This is consistent with the aggregator's "pays off from about 100k".
- **[computed] Effective margin rates on T Premium:**
  - Each bucket's cap works out to 0.068–0.07% per day. For example 70 / 100,000 = 0.07% and 6,800 / 10,000,000 = 0.068%.
  - Annualised, 0.07% per day is **25.6% p.a.** over 365 days or **17.5% p.a.** over 250 trading days. Over 50 mn RUB, 0.055% per day is **20.1%** or **13.8% p.a.**
  - Because the fee is fixed per bucket, a position just above a bucket floor pays roughly double. For example 50,001 RUB pays 70 RUB, which is 0.14% per day.
  - Whether the charge applies on calendar or trading days was not confirmed.
- **[computed] Price ratios:**
  - Russia: a no-fee plan at 0.3% is **5x** Sber's 0.06% and **6x** VTB's 0.05%.
  - Kazakhstan: Freedom's 0.085% local commission is **2.8x** Halyk's 0.03%.
  - Freedom's international plan has a 0.5% ad-valorem fee plus fixed per-order fees. On a USD 1,000 order of 10 shares that is 5 + 0.12 + 1.2 = **USD 6.32, or 0.63%** (illustrative).
- **Pattern for the model:** EM brokers monetise mass clients through high ad-valorem fees on no-fee plans (0.3% in Russia, 0.5% on Freedom's international orders), FX conversion (0.4–1%) and margin (about 15–26% p.a.). Active and affluent clients migrate to subscription or tiered plans (0.01–0.06%). Promotional interest on idle cash (13.5–36.6% p.a.) is a 2026 acquisition tool in Russia as the key rate falls.

### Gaps

- **Depository and custody fees** in Russia were not captured; most mass-market plans are believed to have none, but this is not sourced.
- **FX spreads** as distinct from commissions were not captured for any broker. Since 2024, USD and EUR conversions in Russia are largely over-the-counter.
- **Subscription prices** for T Premium/Private, BCS «Трейдер», Sber «Инвестиционный» and other plans were not captured.
